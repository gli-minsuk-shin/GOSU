## One row for the same email delivered to two accounts

User request: the same email arriving in two accounts shows as two identical Briefing rows; show it
once.

Cross-account merging already existed but required a verified source proof on both copies: a
digest of the canonical raw source plus the preview. That proof is read in the reader's last pass
and is skipped for messages with attachments, bodies Mail has not downloaded, or reads cut short,
so most real copies never qualified. The read-only diagnostic of the newest messages showed the
Gmail and Yonsei copies with matching Message-ID and body lengths.

Change: `sameDeliveredMail` also treats two emails as one delivery when they are in different
accounts and share the same Message-ID link (`message://`) and subject. A verified source match
still merges, and two copies whose verified sources differ are never merged. The merged row keeps
every receiving account in `mailCopies` (at most five). The rule applies where copies are already
merged — before summarization in the mail reader and briefing run, and when saved history is
displayed — so existing rows merge on display once both copies have a stored link. `sameVerifiedMail`
itself, summary-cache reuse and proof rechecks are unchanged.
