## Equal-size "먼저 확인할 내용" cards

User request: the boxes in the AI assistant summary had different sizes; make them the same size.

- `.briefing-assistant-highlights` used `align-items: start`, so every card took its own content
  height: mail cards (sender, account and received time) were taller than paper cards, and agenda
  cards were shortest. The grid now stretches its items and uses `grid-auto-rows: 1fr`, so every
  row takes the tallest card's height and all cards in the summary have one size. Columns were
  already equal (`repeat(auto-fit, minmax(180px, 1fr))`).
- A card is a flex column (was a grid) and the jump hint ("상세 보기 ↓", "Calendar에서 열기 ↗",
  "GOSU 할 일에서 열기 ↗") has `margin-top: auto`, so it sits on the same bottom line in every card.
- The live summary and the History summary share these rules. Title and text clamps (two lines)
  are unchanged, which bounds the tallest card.
