## 논문 요약's right pane is its own conversation

0.58.157 moved the paper conversation into the right pane, and it arrived there as a guest: the
AI 비서 sat in that slot until a paper card's 「AI 질의응답」 asked for the other one. The reader
opens 논문 요약 to talk about papers, so being made to summon the chat first was a step with no
purpose.

On that screen the pane is now the paper conversation. Collapsed, its handle reads **논문 요약 AI**
and pressing it opens that. Leaving the tab hands the slot back to the assistant and the paper chat
stays mounted, so coming back resumes it — unchanged from 0.58.157.

「닫기」 changed meaning with it. It used to hand the slot back to an assistant the reader had not
asked for; now it puts the pane away and leaves the conversation alone.

## The standing explanation is off the screen, not deleted

Four rows of muted text under the heading, saying the same four things on every visit, in a pane
whose whole job is showing a conversation. The text now lives on the heading's own tooltip, so what
it explained — that this is one conversation per screen, that it does not mix with the AI 비서, that
a card's button attaches that paper, and where the model is chosen — is still reachable without
costing the rows.
