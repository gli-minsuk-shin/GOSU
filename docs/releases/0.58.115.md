## Compact space above the AI assistant input

User request (with a screenshot of the GOSU AI panel): the lines between the conversation and the
input waste space. The user chose all three changes.

Three lines stood above the input: the progress line ("선택한 Calendar 일정 조회 · 13초"), which repeated
the "turn active" message just above it; the context line ("현재 요청 시작 문맥 추정 약 10,614 /
1,000,000 · 1% 추정 ⌄"); and a standing two-line notice ("이전 설정의 대화 38개도 복원했습니다. …").

- While a turn runs, the progress line is not shown; the elapsed time is in the running message's
  header ("turn active · 13초") next to the progress text it already showed. Completion and failure
  messages keep their line under the conversation.
- The context meter is a chip ("문맥 1% · 추정 ⌄") in the action row with the send button, next to the
  approval hint. The full sentence is its tooltip and the first line of its details, which open upward
  over the conversation. The buttons keep one-line labels; the chip and hint wrap first.
- The restore notice is a one-line divider (`role="note"`) at the top of the conversation, where the
  restored messages begin, instead of standing above the input.
