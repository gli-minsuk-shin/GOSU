## One colour per kind in "먼저 확인할 내용", and the tasks that were missing

User request: make the email, agenda, paper and task boxes of the "AI ASSISTANT SUMMARY · 먼저 확인할
내용" card different colours, so each kind is recognizable at a glance.

The card marked every kind with the same white box, one green label and a green hover; only the small
label text named the kind. Tasks were not in the card at all: it showed at most three saved email and
paper summaries plus two agenda events, while unfinished GOSU tasks appeared only further down in the
agenda section.

Change:

- Each highlight carries its kind as a class already; the styles now give each kind an accent and a
  tint: email `#4f7396`, papers `#4c7228`, agenda `#8a5d17`, task `#6f5599`. The accent is a 3px left
  border and the kind label's colour, the tint is the box background, and hover keeps the same accent
  as the border instead of turning everything green.
- The history card now also shows up to two unfinished tasks (`upcomingTodos` of the run's snapshot,
  overdue and due first, finished tasks excluded) with their due date and project. Inside GOSU the
  card opens the task in the To-do list; in a plain browser it stays a static box.

Measured in the browser with the real styles: the four labels keep 4.61, 5.20, 5.34 and 5.62 contrast
on their own tint, and each kind renders a different accent colour.
