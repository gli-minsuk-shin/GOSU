## An open paper closes from its left bar

User request: in 논문 요약, like the Briefing tab, clicking the left side of an expanded paper should
collapse it, next to the existing minimize control.

- An open paper (`PaperBriefingDisclosure`) now has a bar on its left edge: a thin light-green line
  while open, a darker 6 px bar on hover or keyboard focus. Clicking it closes that paper and returns
  to its title, the same as "논문 요약 접기" and the Briefing sections' left bar (`BriefingSectionRail`,
  which now takes an optional class). It is a real button labelled "이 논문 접기" and is hidden while the
  paper is closed.
- The paper disclosure is shared, so papers in the Briefing feed get the same bar.
- The bar's styles are scoped to the paper (`details.briefing-paper-disclosure > .briefing-item-rail`):
  the generic `.briefing-insight-card details button` rule had given it padding, a border and a white
  background (22 px wide instead of 12 px) in the first preview.
