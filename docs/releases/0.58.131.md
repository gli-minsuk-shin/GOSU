## Email stayed "조회 미완료" after a restart: Mail was too slow, and every restart began with the slowest request

User report (0.58.130, right after the update and a restart): "여전히 이메일 못 읽어오는데? 뭐가 문제냐?"
with the email section showing "Apple Mail의 계정 응답가 지연되고 있습니다", and "여전히 너무 버벅이는데?".

This failure was not the sign-in code mail fixed in 0.58.130. Apple Mail really did not answer. Evidence
from the unified log and process state only (nothing was sent to Mail, no mail content was read):

- GOSU started at 15:01:47. Its one Mail reader (`osascript`) lived from 15:03:10 to 15:05:50: exactly
  the 160 second limit. Mail logged `com.apple.appleevents … invalid right … (dead:1)` at 15:06:23: it
  answered the request 33 seconds after GOSU had given up, to a port that no longer existed.
- The Mac at that moment: load average 110–170 on 10 cores with 75 % of the CPU idle (threads waiting
  on memory, not on CPU), 16 GB of RAM with 77 MB free, 8 GB of 9 GB swap used. Mail had been open for
  four and a half days and 426 MB of its 428 MB were compressed. The largest consumers were the Claude
  desktop app (2.5 GB renderer of a three-day session plus a helper at 30–96 % CPU), WindowServer,
  Notion, Codex, about 3.4 GB of WebKit tabs and Preview. No build process of this repository was
  left running.
- Nothing tried again: the sealed briefing stores were last written at 15:06:01, and the next
  automatic run was four hours away.

Why restarts make it worse (the defect in GOSU): `AppleMailConnection` keeps Mail's account ids and
mailbox paths only in memory, while a saved scope holds only their fingerprints. After every restart
the first mail access therefore ran `discover`, a walk of every mailbox of every account with two
Apple Events per mailbox (this user: 3 accounts, 33 mailboxes, about 80 events). That request printed
no progress, had no partial result, one hard 160 second limit and no retry, and it ran at the moment
a Mac that has just launched a newly installed app is busiest. A session that stays open skips it,
which matches "works, then breaks again after an update and a restart". The two earlier failed runs
(2026-09-20 20:13, 2026-09-21 08:36) were the sign-in code mail; this one is separate.

- **Saved mailbox locations** (`mail-directory-store.ts`, sealed `mail-directory.v1.enc.json`): for the
  mailboxes of an approved scope, Mail's account id and the mailbox path. `restorePolicyGrant` and
  `resolveMailbox` load them first, so a restarted GOSU reads the approved mailbox directly and asks
  Mail for its mailbox list only when the scope is not covered. Fingerprints are computed again from
  the saved values, so an entry can only ever stand for the mailbox it names. The file is written
  once, not at every briefing; an unreadable file only means Mail is asked again; an older GOSU
  ignores the file.
- **Stale locations**: the reader's `mail_account_missing` / `mail_mailbox_missing` now reach the host.
  When the location came from the saved file, Mail is asked for its mailboxes: a renamed mailbox or
  removed account is reported as `mail_account_refresh_required` (the existing "계정·메일함이 현재 설정과
  다릅니다" message) and forgotten; a location Mail confirms keeps `mail_unavailable`. A discovery only
  forgets a location when the list of its account was complete (not limited, not unavailable).
- **Waiting for Mail is not part of the read**: the reader emits `{type:'answered'}` right after
  Mail's first answer. Until then only `APPLE_MAIL_FIRST_RESPONSE_MS` (300 s) runs and the status line
  says "Apple Mail 응답 대기 중 · 1분 30초째 · Mac이 바쁘면 Mail이 깨어나는 데 몇 분 걸릴 수 있습니다" every 15
  seconds. The first answer starts the unchanged 160 second read deadline (partial results as before).
- **A mailbox walk is stopped for silence, not for being slow**: `discover`/`mailboxes` report every
  mailbox they walk; the reader is stopped after 160 seconds without an answer or 420 seconds in total
  (`APPLE_MAIL_DISCOVERY_DEADLINE_MS`), with the stage it reached (`mail_timeout_mailbox`).
- **A silent Mail is not waited for again and again**: after `mail_timeout_account` the other accounts
  of the scope and the Scholar alert search are not sent to the same silent Mail (three accounts plus
  the search would otherwise wait 30 minutes, past the 20 minute run limit).
- **One more try within the run**: when Mail answered and then stopped (`mail_timeout_mailbox`,
  `_metadata`, `_body`, `mail_unavailable`) and no account could be read, a daily run waits 20 seconds
  and reads once more ("Apple Mail 응답이 끊겨 잠시 후 한 번 더 시도합니다…"). Manual source previews and
  the summary refresh keep their single attempt.
- **Ten minutes, not four hours**: a daily run in which mail stayed unread for one of those reasons
  (all accounts, or some: `delayedAccounts`) returns `retryMailInMs`. `BriefingGeneration.followUpMail`
  moves `nextDueAt` to ten minutes later, once: the follow-up run itself never plans another, a routine
  nothing runs automatically plans nothing, and a schedule paused for changed settings
  (`nextDueAt === null`) is not resumed. The status line adds "Apple Mail은 13:10쯤 자동으로 다시
  확인합니다." Papers, calendar and to-dos of the first run are summarized as before.
- **Message**: "계정 응답가" was a particle error. The stages now read "메일함 응답이 / 메일 목록 읽기가 / 본문
  읽기가 지연되고 있습니다", and a Mail that never answered says so: "Apple Mail이 5분 동안 응답하지 않아
  메일을 읽지 못했습니다. Mac의 메모리가 부족하거나 Mail이 오래 켜져 있으면 Mail이 늦게 깨어납니다. …"

Not changed: the 160 second read deadline and its partial results, the 100 second metadata budget, the
body reader, Mail's index path, and what is read. Nothing new is sent to Mail.
