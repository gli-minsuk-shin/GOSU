## 논문 요약 AI opens in the right-hand pane, in the AI 비서's slot

Reported immediately after 0.58.155 shipped: "논문 요약 AI 는 밑이 아니라 AI 비서 대신에 오른쪽에서
열려야지", then "논문 요약은 AI 비서 chat 여는거랑 별개로 논문 요약 Assistant AI 를 따로 붙여야지".

0.58.155 rendered the conversation as the last element of 논문 요약, under every paper card, and
scrolled the page down to it. That fixed the symptom — a button that looked dead — while leaving the
cause: a conversation does not belong at the bottom of a list. A pane already exists for one.

The conversation now opens in the right-hand pane while 논문 요약 is the open tab. Its state moved
up to `briefing-app`, so the screen only asks (`onOpenChat`) and no longer renders a chat at all.
Opening a paper expands the pane, because a conversation behind a collapsed pane is the same dead
button in a new place.

Leaving 논문 요약 hands the pane back to the AI 비서 and returning resumes the paper conversation.
Neither chat is ever unmounted by the other's turn in the pane — the same keep-it-mounted rule
`RetainedBriefingChats` already follows for routines — so an unsent draft on either side survives.
A conversation kept from another routine is filtered out by `routineId` rather than cleared from an
effect, because clearing would race the papers screen's own mount effect, which is what 0.58.153 and
0.58.155 each had to work around.

The `scrollIntoView` 0.58.155 added is gone with the placement it worked around, and the two tests
whose subject was that placement are deleted rather than quietly edited.

## The same chat, its own model, and no recommendations

"논문 요약 AI 는 AI 비서랑 같은 디자인에 기능을 가지게 해줘. 예를 들면, 파일 첨부라든지."

Measured before changing anything: both panes run the same `BriefingChat`, and the composer's
buttons were already identical — 파일 첨부, 권한 설정, 추천 질문. Nothing was missing. What hid them
was the old panel's height: a short bordered box under the list, where the composer had no room.
Moving the conversation into the pane is what makes the tools usable, and a test now pins that the
paper chat keeps them.

What was genuinely missing is the model. The AI 비서 and the 논문 요약 AI are assigned separately in
설정 → Agent (`briefingAssistant` against `paperChat`), so the header that names the assistant's
model would state something untrue over a paper conversation. `BriefingModelBadge` takes the usage
it should name, the pane shows the paper chat's own model and its busy state, and the assistant's
badge keeps its exact wording — two existing tests pin that string, and reusing the model list's
label ("AI 비서 대화") broke both before the suite caught it.

"paper는 추천 질문, 추천 질문 닫기 이건 빼자" — the suggestion panel and its toolbar toggle are gone
from a paper conversation. They are written for the routine's assistant, and a paper conversation
starts from the paper.
