## Opening an email in Apple Mail marks it read

User request: pressing the open-email button in Briefing should turn the unread mark into read, and
Apple Mail must follow.

- `AppleMailLink` takes `onOpened`, called only after Apple Mail accepted the open request
  (`/mail/open` returned `requested`). The email disclosure then marks an email that is still unread
  (unread at briefing time and not already marked by GOSU) through the shared `markMailReadFor` from
  0.58.118: the same single-message `/mail/mark-read` path with its message check and confirmation
  policy, the status beside the title turning to read, the briefing record updated, and a failure
  reason shown under the status.
- A failed open marks nothing; an email already read, or already marked in this session, is only
  opened.
- Saved briefings and the live briefing view share the email disclosure, so both behave this way.
