## The title bar says what AI is doing

User request: "Local 동기화는 또 뭐냐? 이거 대신에 지금 돌아가고 있는 AI 작업에 대해서 간략히 설명하는
칸으로 만들어줘 … 여러개 AI가 동시에 돌아가면, 1.5초에 한 번씩 바뀌면서 … 클릭하면, 작업중인 chat으로
바로 이동".

- The sync pill is gone. In its place one line names the AI work that is running: the assistant,
  a briefing, paper summaries, a lecture, or a project's chat, Critical Review, experiments or
  literature, named after the project.
- Several at once take turns, one every 1.5 seconds, in a fixed order so the line does not jump
  about. A small count says how many there are.
- Running work turns the same working star the sidebar uses. Work that finished and has not been
  opened shows the yellow star instead, until its screen is opened, exactly as in the sidebar.
- Clicking the line opens the screen that is doing the work, which also marks it as seen. It opens
  the one being shown at that moment, not the first of the list.
- Nothing is shown while nothing is running, so an idle title bar stays quiet.
- The line reads the same state the sidebar stars read, so the two can never disagree. A scope whose
  project is no longer in the workspace is left out rather than shown as an identifier.
- The local sync queue was not thrown away: its count now sits on the "Sync API" row of Settings →
  연결, where the rest of the connection state already lives.

## The quote of the day is about more than the lab

User request: "격언이 너무 연구에 국한될 필요 없음. 삶의 지혜나 유머 위트도 나오게 해줘."

- The instruction no longer describes the reader as a researcher first. It asks for a wide range:
  everyday things, time, money, habits, friends and family, love, food, sleep, travel, failure,
  growing older, learning and plain human folly. Work, research and deadlines are now the one
  subject that is held back, to at most one day in three and never two days running.
- wisdom is no longer restricted to a historical figure: an observation of the model's own counts,
  with author null. The rule against putting invented words in a real person's mouth is unchanged.
- Each day carries a suggested subject, chosen from fourteen by the date, so consecutive days differ
  and the model is not left to its own habits. It is a nudge the model may ignore.
- The built-in list, used whenever the model has not written yet or could not, gained nine lines per
  language about life rather than the lab, so even the fallback is not all about papers.

## The sidebar reads as one rail

User request: "왼쪽 sidebar에 프로젝트, 아카이브 bold 로 하지말자. 그리고 하위 project도 bold로 하지
말고, font size를 상위 보다 조금 작게".

- Nothing in the rail is bold any more. The section names 프로젝트 and 아카이브 stay at the body
  size, and every row under them, a project, its pages and an archived project, reads one step
  smaller. The selected row is what stands out, by colour and its green edge.
