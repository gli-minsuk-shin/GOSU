## Usage: what each feature cost, and a picture of where tokens and money went

User request: "사용량 보여줄때 기능별로 얼마나 썼는지 token 사용량과 함께 돈으로 보여줘. 그리고 model 별,
기능별, 사용량을 token, 사용한 cost 같은 정보를 보여주는 시각화도 넣어줘." The plan was reviewed and
approved first; the user chose the form (two ranked bar lists) and the default comparison (cost).

- **A feature has no price of its own.** Its API-equivalent cost is the sum over the models that ran
  it, so the report has a new optional breakdown `byWorkloadModel` (feature × model, at the grain of
  `byModel`: connection, provider, upstream provider, model). Unlike the project breakdown it keeps
  rows without a project: the AI assistant and the briefing summaries are exactly those. Cut at 1,000
  rows like the other model breakdowns; every filter of the screen narrows it like the rest.
- **One source for every amount** (`buildUsageDistribution`). Cost does not add up across groupings: an
  aggregate loses its cache counts as soon as one reported turn lacks them, and is then priced at the
  full input rate. The summary card, the model cards, both lists and the feature table are therefore
  all priced from the feature-by-model parts, and features, models and the card agree to the cent. The
  parts are used only when their turns add up to the report's total (`usageCostParts`): an older report
  or a cut list prices models from their own rows as before and shows a feature as
  "모델별 내역 없음" instead of a smaller number.
- **An unknown is never zero**: a row's amount is `known` (with the models that had no price named),
  `unpriced` ("가격 미확인"), `unreported` ("—", no turn reported tokens) or `no_breakdown`. Without a
  price list there is no amount, column or switch anywhere, as before.
- **"사용 분포"** (`UsageDistribution`, after the price note, before the model cards): two ranked
  lists, 모델 and 기능. Every row shows the tokens and the amount in fixed columns and one thin bar; the
  switch "토큰 | API 환산" only changes the order, the bar and which number is emphasized. Cost is the
  default comparison, and the choice survives period and filter changes (state lives in `UsageView`;
  the report remounts). Unknown amounts sort last with a dashed track instead of a bar; a feature priced
  only in part says how many models were left out; a model is qualified by its connection only when
  the report has more than one; more than twelve rows end in an explicit "외 N개". Quiet by design:
  caption-size regular text, one color, no animation; the lists stack below 720px.
- Accessibility: a labelled section and two labelled ordered lists, the exact numbers in screen-reader
  text (the compact ones and the bar are hidden from it), a `role="group"` of `aria-pressed` buttons
  (not tabs: the page's three breakdown tabs stay the only ones).
- **Feature table** ("기능별 사용량"): a "합계" column, and "API 환산" when a price list exists
  ("$0.027+" with a tooltip when a model without a price was left out). Its headers moved from
  hard-coded Korean to the message catalog, with `scope="col"`.
- Not in this release: a cost view of the daily trend. It needs a day × model breakdown and a second
  chart builder; tokens × an average price would be a guess, which the price rule forbids.

Checked against real data (copies of `native-usage-ledger.v1.json` and the fetched
`model-prices.v1.json`, numbers only): 375 turns this month in 12 feature-by-model parts, complete;
브리핑·요약 $12.41 (8.87M tokens), AI 비서 $3.92, Model Lab $2.33; features, models and the card all
sum to $18.6573; `gpt-5.3-codex-spark` is "가격 미확인" and one model has turns without reported
tokens ("—"). The encrypted database (Project Chat and other rows) was not read.

Seen in a browser (the real `UsageView` rendered to a static page with example data, 768px viewport the
user had chosen, light and dark): stacked lists by cost, the two-column layout by tokens (page zoomed
to get a wide container), an unpriced model, a 97-character model id (ellipsized, numbers intact), the
same model under two connections, and the view without a price list (no switch, no cost cells, no
"API 환산" text; the token list still there).

Regression tests: `model-usage-contracts.test.ts` (+1: with and without the field, strict rows, 1,001
rows rejected), `model-usage-service.test.ts` (+1: assistant rows included, parts add up to both
marginals and the total, every filter narrows them), `usage-distribution-model.test.ts` (new, 6: a
feature is the sum of its models; agreement with partly missing cache counts; the four cost states;
no price list and incomplete parts; connection naming; ranking and bar share),
`usage-distribution-ui.test.tsx` (new, 5), `usage-view.test.tsx` (+1: default cost comparison, the
table's new columns, one source, tab count unchanged, nothing without a price list), `packages/ui`
`language.test.ts` (+1). The completeness rule caught an inconsistent fixture of mine on its first run.
