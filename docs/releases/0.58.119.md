## Ratings: 24 keywords and the senders of rated emails

User request, after asking how 관심 있음/없음 reach the AI summary: raise the preferred and avoided
keyword lists from 12 to 24 each, and make email ratings count for their sender (address and domain).

How ratings reached the summary before: each rating is a local encrypted memory line (decision, kind,
title and, for papers, their tags). Before each summary batch the routine's ratings become a
personalization profile (counts, a +/−1 score per kind, and the top preferred and avoided keywords,
which come from paper tags only) that the model uses to calibrate importance. An email rating carried
no sender and no keywords, so it only moved the overall email score.

- `feedbackProfile` keeps up to 24 preferred and 24 avoided keywords (`FEEDBACK_PROFILE_LIMIT`). A
  single rating still stores at most 12 of its own keywords.
- Rated emails count for their sender. `feedbackProfile` takes a lookup that finds the sender of a
  rated email by its item id in the saved briefings and the current collection
  (`feedbackSenderLookup`), so ratings saved before this version count too, and no new field is added
  to the memory file (an older GOSU still reads it). Scores are kept per address and per institution
  domain (up to 24 preferred and 24 avoided each); personal webmail domains (gmail.com, naver.com,
  daum.net, outlook.com and others) are not treated as institutions.
- In the email summary, an email whose sender address or institution domain (including subdomains)
  was rated carries `senderFeedback: 'preferred' | 'avoided'`, with an added instruction: preferred
  leans toward higher importance; avoided toward lower importance unless the email itself asks for a
  reply, deadline or decision; never evidence. The sender lists themselves are not sent to the model
  (only each email's own mark), and without rated senders the prompt is unchanged. Email ratings are
  private and, as before, are used only when private AI use is allowed.
- The AI chat's profile is unchanged (no sender lists).
