## No important email may be missed silently

User requirement: not a single important email may be missed. Review of the existing safeguards
found that mail could drop out without any warning: the reader scanned at most 250 messages and 50
seconds of metadata from the top of the mailbox (Gmail costs about 0.6s per message, and All Mail
also holds sent and already summarized mail); mail that repeatedly sat below that reach could age
out of the days window; a body that could not be read produced a title-only summary that was then
excluded forever; and nothing recorded how far a run had actually examined. The user asked to
raise the metadata scan to 100 seconds and implement the recommended procedure.

Changes:

- Scan time: metadata budget 100s (was 50s); the reader process deadline is 160s (was 60s) so the
  link and body passes still run.
- Verified coverage per mailbox: the workspace records `mailCoverage` (routine, account, mailbox,
  `coveredFrom`, `coveredTo`, `gapFrom`). A read starts from the top and goes down to the previous
  coverage minus a 6-hour overlap (for late-synced mail), never below the approved days window. The
  reader reports whether it reached that floor, why it stopped (floor, end, limit, budget, scan cap),
  the oldest message examined, and whether received times were in order.
- Commit after summaries: `nextMailCoverage` advances coverage only through messages that were
  examined in order and handled — summarized, merged into a summarized copy, or not sent to AI by
  the user's settings. A failed summary batch, a body that could not be read, an out-of-order mailbox
  or a read that stopped early leaves a gap. A failed read never advances coverage.
- Gap warnings: every run reports each unexamined interval as "메일 미확인 구간 · account from–to ·
  continued next run", and once, an interval that aged out of the days window with a request to check
  Apple Mail directly. The generation header shows them (`mailCoverageGaps`) even on a completed run.
- Cheaper skips: already summarized deliveries are identified by source id and received time
  (`mailDeliveryKey`) before subject, sender or read status are read, so the budget reaches further.
- Body not read: such summaries are no longer excluded; the mail is read again on the next run and
  the email row shows "본문 미확인 · 원본 확인".
- Serialized reads: the ordinary mail read and the Google Scholar alert search no longer query Apple
  Mail at the same time.
- Counts in the mail note: messages examined, already summarized ones skipped, and whether the read
  reached the previous coverage.
- Merged copies are labeled "같은 메일 · N개 계정 수신" (they can now be merged by Message-ID, not only
  by a verified source).

Not done: mail sent by the user is still read like other mail. Skipping it would need the sender
before the cheap check and could hide notes sent to oneself. Priority of body-less mail is flagged,
not re-ranked, because stored rows do not record whether a preview was requested.
