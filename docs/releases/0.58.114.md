## Wide, low "먼저 확인할 내용" cards

User request: the summary boxes are nearly square and waste space; make them flatter. The user chose
"넓고 낮게" over only widening the columns.

Measured with the real CSS at a 960 px summary, a card was 221 × 185 px (width:height 1.2). Narrow
180 px columns split the mail sender, receiving account and time over two lines, and the jump hint
("상세 보기 ↓") took its own bottom line; since 0.58.113 every card takes the tallest card's height, so
all cards carried that space.

- Columns are at least 260 px (`minmax(min(260px, 100%), 1fr)`, so a container narrower than 260 px
  still fits).
- A card is a two-column grid: the kind label and the jump hint share the first row (the hint in its
  own `auto` column, so a longer hint such as "Calendar에서 열기 ↗" never overlaps the label); every
  other line spans the card.
- In a card, sender, receiving account and time stay on one line; the account is cut with an ellipsis
  first. Title and summary keep their two-line clamps, and all cards keep one equal size.
